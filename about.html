About — CA Study Hub

CA Inter Group I (Jan 2027) ke liye banaya gaya ek chhota sa blog.

CA Study Hub ek simple sa blog hai — CA Inter Group I ke students ke liye, jo Jan 2027 attempt ki taiyaari kar rahe hain. Yahan likha har article ek hi sawaal ka jawab deta hai: "abhi, aaj, kya karna hai?"

Kya milega yahan?

  • Revision plans — last 60 days ka week-by-week plan, teeno subjects ke saath.
  • Subject-wise tareeke — Adv Accounts ke bade chapters fast revise karna, Law ke sections yaad rakhna, Tax ke common mistakes se bachna.
  • Exam hall strategy — 100 marks ka paper 3 ghante me kaise attempt karein.
  • Mock test strategy — RTP, MTP aur PYQ ka sahi istemal.
  • Consistency — last 2 months me routine kaise banaye rakhein.

Kya nahi milega?

  • Koi "pakka pass hone ki guarantee" nahi — aisa daava koi nahi kar sakta.
  • Koi fake topper story ya invented statistics nahi.
  • Koi paid course ka promotion nahi — yahan jo hai, free hai.
