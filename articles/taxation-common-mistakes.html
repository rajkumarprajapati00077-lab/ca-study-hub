Taxation — common mistakes jo marks kha jate hain

CA Inter Group I · 8 min read · Income Tax + GST

Taxation me aksar aisa hota hai ki student ko answer aata hai, phir bhi marks kat jate hain. Wajah hoti hai chhoti-chhoti galtiyan — working note nahi likha, section galat likha, ya question ko dhyaan se nahi padha. Ye 10 galtiyan har attempt me marks khati hain. Inhe abhi se theek kar lo.

1. Working notes nahi likhna

Ye sabse badi galti hai. Tax me working notes marks dete hain — examiner ko tumhara calculation dikhna chahiye. Agar sirf final answer likha aur wo galat ho gaya to zero. Agar working notes sahi hain aur aakhri step me slip ho gayi to bhi step marks milte hain. Har calculation ka working note likho — chahe wo chhota hi kyun na ho.

2. Question ko aadha padhna

"Compute total income" aur "compute tax liability" — ye dono alag questions hain. "Mr. X, a resident individual" aur "Mr. X, a non-resident" — inka answer alag hoga. Question ke pehle 2 lines me jo facts diye hain (residential status, age, income ka nature), unhe underline karo. Question ko 15 minute reading time me dhyaan se padho — ye 15 minute free me milte hain, inka use karo.

3. Residential status galat nikalna

Residential status Income Tax ka pehla step hai — aur yahi galat ho gaya to poora question galat. Basic conditions (182 days, aur 60 days + 365 days in 4 years) aur additional conditions ko ek table me likhkar yaad rakho. Har question me pehle ye tay karo: resident, non-resident, ya resident but not ordinarily resident? Iske baad hi aage badho.

4. Heads of income mix kar dena

Salary, house property, business, capital gains, other sources — har head ke apne rules hain. Common galtiyan:

  • House property me annual value ka calculation galat karna (gross annual value, municipal taxes, standard deduction 30%).
  • Salary me perquisites aur allowances ka treatment bhool jana.
  • Capital gains me indexation kab lagta hai aur kab nahi, ye confuse karna.
  • Business income me disallowed expenses ko add back karna bhool jana.

Har head ka ek-page summary banao — "is head me kya include hota hai, kya deduct hota hai, kya exempt hai."

5. Deductions (Chapter VI-A) bhool jana

Total income nikalne ke baad deductions lagti hain — Sec 80C (1.5 lakh tak), Sec 80D (medical insurance), aur dusri common deductions. Galti ye hoti hai ki student gross total income nikal kar seedha tax calculate karne lagta hai, deductions lagana bhool jata hai. Ek checklist banao: "Gross total income → minus deductions (80C, 80D, etc.) → total income → tax." Har question me is checklist ko follow karo.

6. GST me place of supply confuse karna

GST me sabse zyada marks place of supply aur time of supply par kat-te hain. Basic rule yaad rakho:

  • Goods: jahan delivery hoti hai, wahi place of supply (aam taur par).
  • Services: receiver ka location (B2B me) ya supplier ka location (B2C me, kuch exceptions ke saath).
  • Inter-state vs intra-state: place of supply se tay hota hai ki IGST lagega ya CGST + SGST.

Place of supply ke rules ko ek flow chart me banao — "goods hai ya service? receiver registered hai ya nahi? location kya hai?" — aur har question me is flow chart se jao.

7. ITC ke rules galat lagana

Input Tax Credit me blocked credit (jaise motor vehicle, food, personal use) aur time limit wale rules hain. Common galti: har input par ITC claim kar lena. Blocked credit ki list alag se yaad rakho — ye ek chhoti si list hai, par isse 4–5 marks ka question ban sakta hai.

8. Rates aur limits purane yaad rakhna

Tax ke rates aur limits (jaise 80C ki 1.5 lakh ki limit) har saal same nahi rehte. Jan 2027 attempt ke liye jo rates aur limits applicable hain, unhi ko yaad rakho. Apni notes me har rate ke saath "Jan 2027" likh lo — taaki purana rate yaad aane par confusion na ho.

9. Calculation slips (totalling galti)

Tax ke questions lambe hote hain — 15–20 figures add karne hote hain. Ek totalling galti poora answer bigaad deti hai. Isse bachne ke liye:

  • Figures ko ek column me likho, seedha-seedha.
  • Total karne ke baad ek baar dobara jodo — 30 second lagte hain, par 5 marks bach jate hain.
  • Calculator par bharosa karo, dimaag se jodne ki koshish mat karo.

10. MCQ / case-study questions ko halke me lena

Tax paper me MCQ (case-study based) bhi hote hain. Log inhe "aasan" samajhkar aakhri me chhod dete hain, phir time nahi bachta. MCQ ke liye bhi practice chahiye — RTP aur MTP ke MCQ solve karo, aur unme bhi working notes ki aadat rakho.

Ek aadat jo sab kuch badal deti hai: Har Tax question solve karne se pehle 30 second me ye 3 cheezein likh lo — (1) kya nikalna hai (total income / tax liability / GST?), (2) kaunsa head ya section lagega, (3) kya deductions ya exemptions hain. Ye 30 second tumhe galat direction me jaane se bachayenge.
