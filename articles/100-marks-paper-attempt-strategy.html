100 marks ka paper 3 ghante me attempt karne ki strategy

CA Inter Group I · 7 min read · 2–5 PM, reading 1:45 PM se

100 marks, 3 ghante. Matlab har mark ke liye 1.8 minute. Ye hisaab dimaag me rakhna sabse zaroori hai — kyunki exam me time ka pata hi nahi chalta, aur aakhri 30 minute me pata chalta hai ki 30 marks ka paper bacha hai. Is article me poora plan hai: reading time se lekar aakhri minute tak.

1:45 – 2:00 PM — Reading time (15 minute, free)

Ye 15 minute likhne ke liye nahi, padhne aur plan karne ke liye milte hain. Inka sahi use ye hai:

  • Poora paper ek baar padho — kaunse questions aasan hain, kaunse mushkil, kaunse tumhare strong chapters se hain.
  • Attempt ka order tay karo. Pehle wo questions jo tumhe sabse achhe se aate hain — isse confidence banta hai aur marks pakke hote hain.
  • Har question ke saamne time likh lo — jaise "Q1: 25 min", "Q2: 20 min". Ye tumhara time budget hai.
  • Compulsory question (agar hai) ko pehchan lo — use chhodna nahi hai.

Reading time me likhna allowed nahi hota, par sochna allowed hai. Is time ko waste mat karo — ye 15 minute tumhare attempt ka plan banate hain.

2:00 – 4:45 PM — Likhne ka time (165 minute)

Ab likhna shuru karo. Time ka hisaab is tarah rakho:

KaamTimeNote
Strong questions pehle~90 minJo aata hai use pehle karo — marks pakke karo.
Medium questions~50 minJo aata hai par time lagega — dusre round me.
Mushkil / bache hue~25 minJo nahi aata use bhi attempt karo — kuch na kuch likho, blank mat chhodo.

Har question ke liye time limit

  • 1 mark = 1.8 minute. 20 marks ka question = 36 minute se zyada nahi. 10 marks ka question = 18 minute.
  • 2 minute rule: Agar kisi question me 2 minute se zyada atak gaye ho (kuch samajh nahi aa raha), to use chhodkar agle par jao. Wapas aakar dobara try karna — pehle attempt me atakna time barbaad karna hai.
  • Har question attempt karo. Blank chhodne se zero milta hai, kuch likhne se step marks mil sakte hain. Working notes, format, ya pehla step bhi likh do.

4:45 – 5:00 PM — Aakhri 15 minute (revision)

Ye 15 minute naye question ke liye nahi hain. Inme ye karo:

  • Jo questions adhure reh gaye hain, unhe poora karo (working notes, total, conclusion).
  • Totalling check karo — Tax aur Accounts me totalling galti common hai.
  • Question numbers check karo — kahi galat number to nahi likha.
  • Answer sheet ke pehle page par apna roll number aur details check karo.

Presentation — marks isse bhi milte hain

  • Headings aur sub-headings likho — examiner ko tumhara answer samajhne me aasani hogi.
  • Working notes alag se likho, "Working Note: 1" likhkar — isse examiner ko pata chalta hai ki calculation kahan hai.
  • Important figures underline karo — final answer, total income, tax liability.
  • Haath ki likhai saaf rakho — examiner ko padhne me dikkat hogi to marks kat sakte hain, chahe answer sahi ho.

Target kya rakhein? Har paper me 40% passing hai aur group me 50% aggregate. Iska matlab hai ki tumhe 100 me se 100 lane ki zaroorat nahi — har paper me 50–60 ka solid score kaafi hai. Ek paper me 80 aur dusre me 30 se kaam nahi chalega. Isliye har paper ko equally seriously lo, aur kisi ek paper ke peeche dusre ko mat bhoolo.

Mock me practice karo — exam me nahi

Ye strategy sirf padhne se nahi, practice se aayegi. Mock test me isi time table ko follow karo — 1:45 reading, 2:00 start, 4:45 revision. 2–3 mock ke baad tumhe apna pace pata chal jayega: kaunse type ke question me kitna time lagta hai, aur kahan time bachta hai. Exam hall me pehli baar ye strategy try mat karo — mock me try karo, exam me execute karo.

Sabse badi galti: Ek mushkil question par 40 minute laga dena, aur aasan questions ke liye time nahi bachna. Exam me har question ke marks barabar hote hain — 20 marks ka mushkil question aur 20 marks ka aasan question, dono 20 marks ke hain. Pehle aasan wale karo, mushkil wale baad me.
