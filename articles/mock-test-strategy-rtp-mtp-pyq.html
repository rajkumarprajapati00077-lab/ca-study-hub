Mock test strategy — RTP / MTP / PYQ kaise use karein

CA Inter Group I · 8 min read · ICAI mock series, error log

RTP, MTP, PYQ — teeno ICAI ka official material hai, par teeno ka kaam alag hai. Jo student teeno ko ek hi tarah se use karta hai, wo teeno ka fayda nahi utha pata. Is article me teeno ka sahi use, mock dene ka tareeka, aur mock ke baad analysis ka system hai.

PYQ — pattern samajhne ke liye

PYQ se tumhe pata chalta hai ki exam me kis type ke questions aate hain, kaunse chapters se zyada questions aate hain, aur question ka level kya hota hai. PYQ ko chapter-wise solve karo — jaise Amalgamation padh rahe ho to uske PYQ questions solve karo. Isse pata chalega ki us chapter se kis tarah ke questions aate hain.

MTP — exam simulation ke liye

MTP ko full paper ki tarah do — 3 ghante, exam time (2–5 PM), bina phone, bina break. MTP ka maksad ye hai ki tum exam hall ka pressure mehsoos karo aur apna time management test karo. MTP ke marks se zyada important hai time ka hisaab — kaunse question me kitna time laga, kahan atak gaye.

RTP — latest trends ke liye

RTP me latest amendments aur is attempt ke liye important topics hote hain. RTP ko aakhri 3–4 hafton me padho — isse pata chalega ki is baar exam me kya naya aa sakta hai. RTP ke questions ko solve karo, par inhe mock ki tarah mat do — inhe study material ki tarah padho.

Mock kaise dein — 5 rules

  1. Exam time par do. 2–5 PM (ya ICAI mock ke liye 10 AM–1 PM). Isse body clock exam time par set hoti hai.
  2. Exam conditions me do. Phone band, table saaf, 3 ghante bina uthe. Aadha mock dene se kuch nahi hota.
  3. Answer sheet par likho. Rough notebook me nahi, proper answer sheet format me — isse presentation ki bhi practice hoti hai.
  4. Time note karo. Har question ke saamne kitna time laga, ye likho. Isse pata chalega kahan time waste hota hai.
  5. Usi din check karo. Mock dekar 2 din baad check karne se fayda nahi — usi din ya agle din subah tak check karo, jab tak questions dimaag me hain.

Mock ke baad analysis — sabse important step

Mock dena aasan hai, analysis karna mushkil — aur analysis hi asli fayda hai. Har mock ke baad ye karo:

  • Error log banao. Ek notebook ya sheet me likho: kaunsa question galat hua, kyun galat hua (concept nahi aata / calculation slip / time nahi mila / question galat padha). Ye 4 categories hi kaafi hain.
  • Weak chapters ki list update karo. Jis chapter se 2 baar galti hui, wo weak chapter hai — use agle hafte dobara karo.
  • Time analysis karo. Kaunse question me zyada time gaya? Kya tumne mushkil question par zyada time laga diya aur aasan chhoot gaya?
  • Marks ka trend dekho. Pehla mock 35, dusra 42, teesra 48 — ye trend upar ja raha hai to tum sahi direction me ho. Ek mock ke marks se kuch decide mat karo, trend dekho.

Kitne mock dene chahiye? Last 60 din me har subject ke kam se kam 3–4 full mocks do — ICAI ki 2 series (6 papers) plus apne MTP/PYQ se 2–3 aur. Zyada mock dene se behtar hai kam mock dekar unki achhi analysis karna. 10 mock bina analysis ke, 3 mock achhi analysis ke barabar nahi hain.

Galti jo sab karte hain: Mock ke marks dekhkar demotivate ho jana. Mock ke marks exam ke marks nahi hote — mock isliye hota hai ki galtiyan abhi pata chalein, exam me nahi. Agar mock me 35 aaye to ye achhi baat hai — tumhe pata chal gaya kahan kaam karna hai. Mock me 70 aakar khush hona aur analysis na karna, mock me 35 aakar analysis karne se bura hai.
