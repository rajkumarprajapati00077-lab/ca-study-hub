CA Inter Group I — last 60 days revision plan

Jan 2027 attempt · 8 min read · Adv Accounts, Law, Taxation

Exam se 60 din pehle sabse badi galti ye hoti hai ki student naya padhne lagta hai. Last 60 days naye chapters ke liye nahi hote — ye revision + practice ke din hote hain. Jo syllabus bacha hai use pehle 2 hafton me nipatao, uske baad sirf revision, mocks aur weak areas.

Pehle dates dimaag me bitha lo:

Sab papers 2–5 PM hain, reading time 1:45 PM se. Gap ke din revision ke liye milte hain — unhe waste mat karna, unka plan pehle se bana lo.

Plan ka structure — 3 phases

PhaseDinKaam
Phase 1 — Finish + 1st revisionDay 60–40Bacha hua syllabus khatam karo, saath me pehli revision. Har subject ka ek round poora hona chahiye.
Phase 2 — 2nd revision + mocksDay 40–20Dusri revision, RTP/MTP/PYQ solve karo, hafte me ek full mock (exam time par).
Phase 3 — Final revisionDay 20–0Sirf short notes + weak chapters + full mocks. Naya kuch mat chhedo.

Phase 1 (Day 60–40): syllabus khatam + pehli revision

  • Roz 3 subjects, rotation se. Ek din sirf ek subject mat padho — teeno ko roz chhuo, chahe 2–2 ghante hi kyun na ho. Isse koi subject 4–5 din tak chhoota nahi rehta.
  • Bache hue chapters ki list banao. Har subject me jo chapters bache hain, unhe likho aur date laga do. List bina date ke sirf kagaz hai.
  • Revision notes saath me banao. Jo padh rahe ho, uska ek-page summary ya formula sheet usi din banao. Aakhri dinon me yahi notes kaam aayenge, poori kitab nahi.
  • Roz ka target: 8–10 ghante padhai (breaks alag). 12 ghante ka plan banakar 6 ghante padhne se behtar hai 9 ghante ka plan banakar 9 ghante padhna.

Phase 2 (Day 40–20): dusri revision + mocks

  • Dusri revision pehli se tez hogi. Pehli revision me 3 din lagne wala chapter dusri me 1 din me ho jana chahiye — kyunki ab tumhe pata hai kya aata hai aur kya nahi.
  • RTP / MTP / PYQ shuru karo. Pehle PYQ (past papers) — isse pattern samajh aata hai. Phir MTP — exam jaisi practice. RTP aakhri me — latest trends aur amendments ke liye.
  • Hafte me ek full mock, exam time par. 2–5 PM, 3 ghante, bina phone, bina break. Mock dene ke baad usi din analysis karo — kaunse question me time gaya, kahan marks kate.
  • Weak chapters ki list alag rakho. Mock me jo chapter weak nikla, use agle 2 din me dobara karo. Weak list chhoti hoti jayegi — yahi progress hai.

Phase 3 (Day 20–0): final revision

  • Sirf short notes. Ab poori kitab kholne ka time nahi hai. Formula sheets, section lists, aur apne ek-page summaries se revise karo.
  • Full mocks, exam conditions me. 2–5 PM wale slot me mock do. Isse body clock bhi exam time par set hoti hai.
  • Gap days ka plan pehle se likho. 3 Jan (Sun) ko Law revise karna hai, 5 Jan (Tue) ko Taxation — ye pehle se tay karo, us din sochne me time waste mat karo.
  • Naya kuch mat chhedo. Aakhri 10 din me koi naya chapter, naya book, naya YouTube video nahi. Jo aata hai use pakka karo.

Yaad rakhna: 60 din ka plan kagaz par perfect lagta hai, asli kaam roz follow karna hai. Agar ek din plan chhoot jaye to agle din double karne ki koshish mat karo — bas wapas track par aa jao. Ek din chhootna haar nahi hai, 5 din chhootna hai.

Roz ka sample timetable

  • Subah (3 ghante): Adv Accounts — numerical practice + ek chapter revision.
  • Dopahar (3 ghante): Law — theory + section revision + likhkar practice.
  • Shaam (3 ghante): Taxation — Income Tax ya GST, working notes ke saath.
  • Raat (1 ghanta): Din bhar ka revision — jo padha use 15 minute me dobara dohrao, aur agle din ka plan likho.

Ye sirf ek sample hai — apne hisaab se badlo, par teeno subjects roz ka rule mat todo.

Passing ka hisaab: Har paper me 40% aur group me kul 50% aggregate chahiye hota hai. Iska matlab hai ki tumhe har paper me 90 lane ki zaroorat nahi — 50–60 ka solid score har paper me, aggregate 50% ke upar, kaafi hai. Ek paper me 80 aur dusre me 30 se kaam nahi chalega.
