CA Inter Group I — the last 60 days revision plan

Jan 2027 attempt · 8 min read · Advanced Accounting, Law, Taxation

The biggest mistake students make 60 days before the exam is starting something new. The last 60 days are not for new chapters — they are for revision and practice. Finish whatever syllabus is left in the first two weeks, then only revision, mocks and weak areas.

First, fix the dates in your mind:

PaperDateGap
Advanced AccountingSat, 2 Jan 2027—
LawMon, 4 Jan 20271 day (3 Jan, Sun)
TaxationWed, 6 Jan 20271 day (5 Jan, Tue)

All papers are 2–5 PM, with reading time from 1:45 PM. The gap days are for revision — don't waste them; plan them in advance.

The plan — 3 phases

PhaseDaysWork
Phase 1 — Finish + 1st revisionDay 60–40Finish the remaining syllabus, along with the first revision. One full round of every subject should be done.
Phase 2 — 2nd revision + mocksDay 40–20Second revision, solve RTP/MTP/PYQ, and take one full mock every week (at exam time).
Phase 3 — Final revisionDay 20–0Only short notes, weak chapters and full mocks. Don't start anything new.

Phase 1 (Day 60–40): finish the syllabus + first revision

  • Study all 3 subjects every day, in rotation. Don't study only one subject in a day — touch all three, even if it is just 2 hours each. This way no subject stays untouched for 4–5 days.
  • Make a list of the chapters that are left. Write down the pending chapters in every subject and put a date against each one. A list without a date is just paper.
  • Make revision notes as you go. Whatever you study, make a one-page summary or formula sheet the same day. In the last days, these notes are what you will use — not the whole book.
  • Daily target: 8–10 hours of study (excluding breaks). It is better to plan 9 hours and study 9 hours than to plan 12 hours and study 6.

Phase 2 (Day 40–20): second revision + mocks

  • The second revision will be faster than the first. A chapter that took 3 days in the first revision should take 1 day in the second — because now you know what you already understand and what you don't.
  • Start RTP / MTP / PYQ. Start with PYQ (past papers) — they show you the pattern. Then MTP — exam-like practice. RTP last — for the latest trends and amendments.
  • One full mock every week, at exam time. 2–5 PM, 3 hours, no phone, no break. Analyse the mock the same day — which question took time, where you lost marks.
  • Keep a separate list of weak chapters. A chapter that comes out weak in a mock should be revised again in the next 2 days. The weak list getting shorter is real progress.

Phase 3 (Day 20–0): final revision

  • Only short notes. There is no time to open the full book now. Revise from formula sheets, section lists and your own one-page summaries.
  • Full mocks in exam conditions. Take mocks in the 2–5 PM slot. This also sets your body clock to exam time.
  • Plan the gap days in advance. Decide now that 3 Jan (Sun) is for Law revision and 5 Jan (Tue) is for Taxation — don't waste time deciding on the day itself.
  • Don't start anything new. No new chapter, no new book, no new YouTube video in the last 10 days. Make what you already know solid.

Remember: a 60-day plan looks perfect on paper — the real work is following it every day. If you miss one day, don't try to do double the next day — just get back on track. Missing one day is not failure; missing five days is.

A sample daily timetable

  • Morning (3 hours): Advanced Accounting — numerical practice + revision of one chapter.
  • Afternoon (3 hours): Law — theory + section revision + writing practice.
  • Evening (3 hours): Taxation — Income Tax or GST, with working notes.
  • Night (1 hour): Revise the day — go over what you studied in 15 minutes, and write tomorrow's plan.

This is only a sample — change it to suit yourself, but don't break the rule of studying all three subjects every day.

The passing rule: you need 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate in the group. That means you don't need 90 in every paper — a solid 50–60 in each paper, with aggregate above 50%, is enough. An 80 in one paper and a 30 in another will not work.
