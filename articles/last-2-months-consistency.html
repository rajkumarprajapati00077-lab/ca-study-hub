Consistency

Last 2 months me consistency kaise rakhein

CA Inter Group I · 7 min read · Routine, discipline, burnout

Last 2 months me sabse zyada students isliye peeche reh jate hain kyunki wo consistent nahi reh pate — ek din 12 ghante padhte hain, phir 3 din kuch nahi. Motivation ek din ki cheez hai, routine roz ki. Is article me routine banane ka practical tareeka hai — koi gyaan nahi, sirf kaam ki baatein.

1. Fixed routine banao — aur use likh lo

Routine dimaag me nahi, kagaz par hona chahiye. Ek simple timetable banao:

  • Uthne aur sone ka fixed time. Roz same time par utho aur so jao — body clock set hogi to subah padhne me aasani hogi.
  • 3 study blocks. Subah, dopahar, shaam — har block 2.5–3 ghante ka. Har block me ek subject.
  • Breaks bhi likho. Har 50 minute ke baad 10 minute ka break, aur khane ka time alag se. Break bina likhe routine adhura hai.

Routine ko realistic rakho. 14 ghante ka routine banakar 6 ghante padhne se behtar hai 9 ghante ka routine banakar 9 ghante padhna. Routine wo hai jo follow ho, wo nahi jo kagaz par achha lage.

2. Phone discipline — sabse bada dushman

Phone se 1 ghanta nikalna aasan hai, 1 ghanta wapas lana mushkil. Iske liye:

  • Study time me phone dusre room me rakho. Silent par rakhna kaafi nahi hai — phone dikhna bhi distraction hai.
  • Social media ke liye fixed time rakho. Din me 30 minute, shaam ko — bas. Isse zyada nahi.
  • Study apps ka use karo. Timer app (Pomodoro) se 50 minute ka block set karo — timer chal raha hai to phone chhoone ka mann kam hota hai.

3. Kharab din ka plan pehle se banao

60 din me 5–6 din aise aayenge jab padhne ka mann nahi karega — thakan, low mood, ya koi pareshani. Un dinon ke liye minimum plan pehle se tay karo:

  • Minimum 2 ghante. Kharab din par bhi 2 ghante padho — bas revision, koi naya chapter nahi. 2 ghante se routine tootta nahi hai.
  • Sirf revision karo. Kharab din par naya mat padho — purana revise karo. Isse progress bhi hoti hai aur pressure bhi nahi padta.
  • Khud ko dosh mat do. Ek kharab din se kuch nahi bigadta. Agle din wapas routine par aa jao — bas. 5 kharab din ek saath aaye to problem hai, 1 kharab din nahi.

4. Burnout se bachna

Burnout tab hota hai jab tum bina break ke hafte bhar padhte ho. Isse bachne ke liye:

  • Hafte me aadha din off rakho. Hafte me ek dopahar ya shaam — revision halki karo, ya bilkul mat karo. Dimaag ko rest chahiye hota hai.
  • Neend poori lo. 7 ghante ki neend zaroori hai. Raat ko 2 baje tak padhkar subah 6 baje uthna consistency nahi, burnout hai.
  • Khana aur paani. Basic hai, par exam time me log bhool jate hain. Theek se khao, paani piyo — dimaag ko fuel chahiye hota hai.
  • Exercise ya walk. Roz 20–30 minute walk ya halki exercise — isse neend achhi aati hai aur dimaag fresh rehta hai.

5. Progress track karo — roz

Consistency banaye rakhne ka sabse aasan tareeka hai progress dekhna. Roz raat ko 2 minute me ye likho:

  • Aaj kitne ghante padhe?
  • Kya-kya complete hua (chapter / revision / mock)?
  • Kal kya karna hai?

Ye 2 minute ka kaam tumhe roz batayega ki tum aage badh rahe ho. Aur jab progress dikhti hai to motivation apne aap aati hai — bahar se motivation dhoondhne ki zaroorat nahi padti.

Yaad rakhna: Consistency ka matlab roz 12 ghante padhna nahi hai. Consistency ka matlab hai roz padhna — chahe 6 ghante ho ya 10 ghante. 60 din me roz 8 ghante padhne wala student, 10 din 14 ghante aur 10 din 0 ghante padhne wale student se hamesha aage rahega. Slow aur steady hi jeet ta hai.

Last 10 din — kya badlega?

  • Padhai ka time same rakho — achanak 14 ghante karne ki koshish mat karo, body thak jayegi.
  • Naya kuch mat chhedo. Sirf short notes, formula sheets, aur weak chapters.
  • Exam ka saman pehle se tayyar rakho. Admit card, pen, calculator, watch — exam se ek din pehle sab check kar lo.
  • Neend ka khaas dhyaan rakho. Exam se pehle wali raat ko jaldi so jao — raat bhar jaagkar padhne se exam me dimaag kaam nahi karta.
