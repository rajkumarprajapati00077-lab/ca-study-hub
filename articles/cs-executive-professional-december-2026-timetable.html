CS Executive & Professional Dec 2026 — full timetable, 21–28 Dec

CS Executive & Professional · 6 min read · Paper-wise dates, timings, admit card

The CS December 2026 exams are five weeks away. ICSI released the timetable on 4 August 2026, and nothing in it has changed since — Executive and Professional both run from Monday, 21 December to Monday, 28 December 2026, from 2:00 PM to 5:15 PM every day. There is no exam on Friday, 25 December. Here is the full plan.

CS Executive — paper-wise timetable (Syllabus 2022)

Notice the pattern: the papers alternate between groups, and the one-day gap on 25 December falls right in the middle. Use that gap for Tax Laws — it is the only paper after the break, and it is also one of the heaviest.

CS Professional — same dates

Professional runs on the same dates, 21–28 December, at the same time, 2:00–5:15 PM. The first paper on 21 December is Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) — Principles and Practice (Group 1). The last two days are the open-book electives: Elective Paper 2 on Sunday, 27 December and Elective Paper 1 on Monday, 28 December. Elective choices include CSR and Social Governance, Internal and Forensic Audit, Intellectual Property Rights, AI / Data Analytics / Cyber Security, GST and Corporate Tax Planning, and Arbitration, Mediation and Conciliation. Check the full paper-wise list on icsi.edu before you plan — do not rely on a forwarded timetable.

Admit card — download it before the exam

ICSI issues admit cards online only, through the SMASH portal at smash.icsi.edu. Download it with your 17-digit registration number, check your name, photograph, signature and exam centre, and carry a printed copy to the centre. ICSI does not send a hard copy. If your centre or paper details look wrong, raise it on the portal immediately — do not wait till 20 December.

The 25 December gap is free revision time. Do not treat it as a holiday. Revise Tax Laws on the 25th, sleep early, and walk into the 27 December paper fresh. Students who waste the gap usually say Tax was the paper they were least ready for.

CSEET — February 2027 and June 2027

If you are starting CS now, the next CSEET is 1–4 February 2027. Registration for the June 2027 CSEET opened on 1 November 2026 and closes on 28 February 2027. Register on icsi.edu — do not wait for the last week, because document upload problems in the last week are the most common reason students miss the window.

Your last 5 weeks — what to do now

  1. This week (14–20 Nov): finish any pending chapters. No new chapter after 20 November.
  2. 21 Nov – 10 Dec: first full revision + one mock per group. Write the mistakes in an error log.
  3. 11–20 Dec: second revision, only short notes and your error log. Download the admit card and check your centre.
  4. 21–28 Dec: exam days. Revise only the next day's paper the evening before — never start a new topic during the exam week.

Result: the December 2026 result is expected in February 2027. Do not believe any result date shared on social media until ICSI posts it on icsi.edu.
