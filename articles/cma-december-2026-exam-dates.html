CMA Dec 2026 — Foundation 13 Dec, Inter & Final 10–17 Dec

CMA Foundation, Inter & Final · 5 min read · Dates, timings, result dates

CMA December 2026 is four weeks away. ICMAI published the timetable on 13 August 2026, and the exam form window for this session has already closed — so if you are registered, your only job now is revision. Foundation is a single day, 13 December. Intermediate and Final run for eight days, 10–17 December. Here are the dates exactly as ICMAI announced them.

Intermediate and Final follow the same pattern: Group I papers fall on 10, 12, 14 and 16 December, and Group II papers on 11, 13, 15 and 17 December — one paper per day, no gap day. Final students sit in the morning, Intermediate students in the afternoon.

What falls on which day

For Intermediate, the first paper on 10 December is Business Laws and Ethics. For Final, the first paper on 10 December is Corporate and Economic Laws. The last day, 17 December, is Management Accounting for Intermediate and the Electives for Final — Strategic Performance Management and Business Valuation, Risk Management in Banking and Insurance, or Entrepreneurship and Start-up. Download the full paper-wise timetable from icmai.in and pin it above your desk; your paper order decides your study order.

Admit card

Admit cards are expected in the last week of November 2026, about two weeks before the first paper. Download yours from icmai.in using your login, check your name and centre, and carry a printed copy. If anything looks wrong, contact ICMAI immediately — do not leave it till 9 December.

One paper a day means one revision a night. With no gap days, you cannot revise a paper after its exam. Finish each paper's revision the night before it, and use the morning (for Inter) or the evening (for Final) only for short notes. The students who struggle most in CMA December are the ones who try to revise two papers in one day.

Your last 4 weeks

  1. 14–23 Nov: finish pending chapters. No new chapter after 23 November.
  2. 24 Nov – 5 Dec: first full revision + one mock per group. Write every mistake in an error log.
  3. 6–9 Dec: second revision from short notes and the error log only. Download the admit card and check your centre.
  4. 10–17 Dec: exam days. Revise only tomorrow's paper each evening.

If you missed the December form: the form window closed in October. The next session is June 2027. Do not pay anyone who promises a late form — ICMAI does not take forms after the window closes. Use the next six months properly instead of chasing a shortcut.
