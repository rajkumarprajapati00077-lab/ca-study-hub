Law — sections yaad rakhne ka tareeka

CA Inter Group I · 7 min read · Companies Act, LLP, FEMA

Law me sabse badi shikayat ek hi hai — "provision yaad hai, section number bhool jata hoon." Aur exam me section number likhna marks deta hai. Iska hal ratta maarna nahi hai, hal hai system — section ko chapter se jodna, phir provision se, phir ek chhoti si story se.

Step 1 — Chapter-wise section map banao

Law ke 15 chapters hain. Har chapter ke important sections ki ek list banao — ek page par, chapter ka naam aur uske 4–6 important sections. Jaise:

  • Share Capital & Debentures: Sec 43 (kinds of share capital), Sec 52 (securities premium), Sec 53 (discount par shares nahi), Sec 54 (sweat equity), Sec 55 (preference shares).
  • Management & Administration: Sec 149 (board of directors), Sec 164 (disqualification), Sec 165 (directorship ki limit), Sec 177 (audit committee).
  • Acceptance of Deposits: Sec 73 (deposits accept karne par rok), Sec 74 (repayment), Sec 76 (public deposits).
  • Accounts & Audit: Sec 128 (books of account), Sec 129 (financial statements), Sec 139 (auditor ki appointment), Sec 143 (auditor ke powers).

Ye map ek baar ban gaya to tumhe pata hoga ki kaunsa section kis chapter ka hai — aur exam me section likhte time chapter yaad aayega to section bhi yaad aayega.

Step 2 — "Section + Provision + Example" ka trio

Section number akele ratne se nahi yaad hota. Har section ko teen cheezon se jodo:

  • Section number: jaise Sec 185.
  • Provision (ek line me): "Company director ko loan nahi de sakti (kuch exceptions ke saath)."
  • Ek example: "Agar company ne apne director ko 10 lakh ka loan diya to Sec 185 lagega."

Jab tum section ko ek example se jodte ho to dimaag me ek picture ban jati hai — aur picture number se zyada der tak yaad rehti hai. Exam me example yaad aayega to section number bhi yaad aayega.

Step 3 — Story method (sequence yaad rakhne ke liye)

Kuch sections ek sequence me aate hain — jaise share capital ke sections (43, 52, 53, 54, 55) ya meeting ke sections. Inhe ek chhoti si kahani me bandh lo. Jaise: "Company ne pehle share capital banaya (43), phir premium liya (52), discount par nahi diya (53), sweat equity di (54), aur preference shares issue kiye (55)." Kahani ajeeb ho to bhi chalega — ajeeb kahani zyada yaad rehti hai.

Step 4 — Roz 30 minute ki section revision

Law ki revision ka sabse asaan tareeka hai roz 30 minute, sirf sections. Isme ye karo:

  • Ek chapter ka section map kholo, aur bina dekhe sections likho. Jo bhool gaye use tick karo.
  • Bhoole hue sections ko usi din dobara padho — agle din phir test karo.
  • Har hafte ek baar saare chapters ke section maps ko ek saath dohrao.

30 minute zyada nahi hai, par roz karne se 60 din me 30 ghante ki section revision ho jayegi — aur yehi 30 ghante exam me section numbers dilayenge.

LLP, GCA aur FEMA ko alag se dekho: Companies Act ke alawa LLP Act 2008, General Clauses Act 1897, Interpretation of Statutes aur FEMA 1999 bhi syllabus me hain. Inhe aakhri me mat chhodo — inke liye hafte me 2–3 ghante alag se rakho. Ye chapters chhote hain, par inke marks bhi hain.

Likhkar practice — Law me bhi zaroori hai

Law theory subject hai, isliye log sirf padhte hain, likhte nahi. Ye galti hai. Exam me answer likhna hota hai — provision, section, aur conclusion, sahi order me. Hafte me kam se kam 2–3 answers likhkar practice karo:

  • Format: Pehle provision (section ke saath), phir facts par apply karo, phir conclusion.
  • Case-based questions: Pehle issue pehchano (kaunsa section lagega), phir provision likho, phir conclusion.
  • Time: Ek answer 8–10 minute me likhne ki practice karo — exam me isse zyada time nahi milega.

Galti jo sab karte hain: Sirf bare act ya sirf ek book se padhna. Law me ek hi source se padho aur use baar-baar revise karo. 3 sources se ek baar padhne se behtar hai 1 source se 3 baar padhna. Source badalna revision nahi hai, confusion hai.
