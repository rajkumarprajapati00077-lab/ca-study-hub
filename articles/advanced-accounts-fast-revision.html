Advanced Accounts — bade chapters fast revise karne ka tareeka

CA Inter Group I · 7 min read · Amalgamation, Consolidation, Partnership

Adv Accounts me sabse badi problem ye hai ki chapters bade hain aur har chapter me 10–15 tarah ke questions hote hain. Revision ke time poora chapter dobara padhna possible nahi hai. Isliye revision ka tareeka alag hona chahiye — format pehle, adjustments dusre, questions aakhri me.

Step 1 — Har bade chapter ka "format" yaad rakho

Adv Accounts ke questions ka ek fixed format hota hai. Agar format yaad hai to aadha question ban jata hai. Har bade chapter ka format ek page par likh lo:

  • Amalgamation: Purchase consideration ka calculation, journal entries, aur transferee company ki balance sheet ka format. Ye teen cheezein ek page par.
  • Consolidation (CFS): Consolidated balance sheet ka format — goodwill/capital reserve, minority interest, aur inter-company adjustments ki jagah. Format yaad ho to figures bharna aasan hai.
  • Partnership: Admission, retirement, death aur dissolution — har ek ka alag format hai. Har format ke 4–5 key steps likh lo.
  • Buyback, Internal Reconstruction, Liquidation: Inka bhi ek fixed flow hota hai — pehle kya karna hai, phir kya. Flow ek page par.

Format yaad rakhne ka sabse aasan tareeka hai khud se likhna. Kitab se dekhkar ek baar likho, phir bina dekhe dobara likho. Jo bina dekhe likh liya, wo yaad hai.

Step 2 — "Golden adjustments" ki list banao

Har chapter me 4–5 adjustments aise hote hain jo baar-baar aate hain aur jinme sabse zyada marks kat-te hain. Unki list banao:

  • Amalgamation: Purchase consideration me kya include hota hai aur kya nahi, amalgamation expenses ka treatment, aur statutory reserves ka transfer.
  • Consolidation: Pre-acquisition aur post-acquisition profits ka alag karna, unrealised profit on stock, aur minority interest ka calculation.
  • Partnership: Goodwill ka treatment (admission/retirement par), revaluation ka effect, aur gainer/sacrificer ka hisaab.

Ye list tumhare liye exam se pehle ki checklist hai — question solve karne se pehle is list ko ek baar dekh lo, to common adjustments chhootenge nahi.

Step 3 — Ek chapter, 2–3 questions, bas

Revision me har chapter ke 10 questions solve karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. 2–3 achhe questions kaafi hain — bas wo questions aise hone chahiye jinme chapter ke saare important adjustments aa jayein.

  • Pehla question: basic — format aur simple adjustments wala.
  • Dusra question: tricky — jisme 2–3 adjustments ek saath hon.
  • Teesra question (agar time ho): past paper ya MTP se — exam jaisa.

Questions ko time par solve karo. Revision me bhi ghadi dekhkar baitho — exam me har question ke liye limited time milega, aur bina practice ke time ka andaza nahi hota.

Step 4 — Ek-page summary har chapter ka

Har bade chapter ka ek-page summary banao — isme ye hona chahiye:

  • Chapter ka format (chhota sa, points me).
  • Golden adjustments (4–5, ek line me har ek).
  • Ek formula ya rule jo hamesha bhoolte ho.
  • Ek question ka naam jo tumne solve kiya tha aur jisme sab kuch aa gaya tha.

Ye ek-page summary aakhri dinon me kaam aayega — poora chapter dobara padhne ki zaroorat nahi padegi, sirf ye page padhna kaafi hoga.

Time ka hisaab: Ek bada chapter (jaise Amalgamation) ki fast revision me 2–3 ghante lagne chahiye — 30 minute format, 30 minute adjustments, 1 ghanta questions, 30 minute summary. Agar ek chapter me 5 ghante lag rahe hain to tum revision nahi, dobara padhai kar rahe ho. Wapas format par aao.

Chhote chapters ko mat bhoolo

Bade chapters par dhyaan dete-dete chhote chapters (jaise Underwriting, ESOP, ya Accounting Standards ke basics) chhoot jate hain. Chhote chapters kam time me zyada marks de sakte hain — unke liye alag se 1–1 ghanta nikaalo. Ek chhota chapter jo poora aata hai, ek bade chapter ke aadhe hisse se zyada marks de sakta hai.

Galti jo sab karte hain: Revision me sirf dekhna aur samajhna — likhna nahi. Adv Accounts likhkar hi yaad hota hai. Jo question bina likhe "samajh" liya, wo exam me likhte time atak jayega. Har revision me kam se kam 2 questions poori tarah likhkar solve karo.
