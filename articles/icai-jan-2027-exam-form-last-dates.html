CA Inter Jan 2027 — exam form closes 19 Nov with late fee, mock tests start 19 Nov

CA Inter Group I · 6 min read · Form deadline, correction window, MTP dates

If you are appearing for CA Inter in January 2027, this week has three deadlines that decide whether you sit in the exam hall at all. The exam form closes on Thursday, 19 November, with a late fee (the no-late-fee date, Monday 16 November, has passed). The mock test series starts on Thursday, 19 November. And if you gave Foundation in September, your result is already out. Here is everything, with the dates as ICAI announced them.

The exam form — do it before 19 Nov

ICAI announced the January 2027 exam schedule on 23 September 2026. The form is filled online only, on the Self Service Portal at eservices.icai.org. There is no offline form.

WhatDate
Form window openedTue, 3 Nov 2026
Last date without late feeMon, 16 Nov 2026
Last date with late fee (Rs 600)Thu, 19 Nov 2026
Correction window (city / medium)Fri, 20 Nov – Sun, 22 Nov 2026

Do not leave the form for 19 November. The late fee is only Rs 600, but a last-day server error or a wrong photo/signature upload costs you the whole attempt. Fill it today, then use the correction window only if you actually made a mistake.

Group II runs 8, 10 and 12 January. Foundation runs 3, 5, 7 and 9 January. ICAI has said the schedule will not change even if a public holiday is declared on an exam day.

Mock tests — Series I starts 19 Nov

ICAI's Board of Studies announced the mock test papers on 14 October 2026. Both series run in physical mode at ICAI branches and in virtual mode online. Papers are uploaded by 9:30 AM on the test day, and the answer key comes within 48 hours.

SeriesAdv AccountsLawTaxation
Series IThu, 19 Nov 2026Sat, 21 Nov 2026Mon, 23 Nov 2026
Series IIThu, 3 Dec 2026Sat, 5 Dec 2026Mon, 7 Dec 2026

Series I runs 19–30 November and Series II runs 3–14 December, covering all six Inter papers. Register on the BoS MTP portal at bosmtp.icai.org and download papers from the BoS Knowledge Portal at boslive.icai.org. For physical mode, contact your local ICAI branch in advance — do not just turn up on the day.

How to use the two series: take Series I at exam time (10 AM–1 PM) and check it the same evening. The gap between Series I and Series II is your fix window — every mistake in Series I becomes a revision topic before Series II. Two series with proper analysis beat five random mocks with no analysis.

Foundation September 2026 result is out

ICAI declared the CA Foundation September 2026 result on 6 November 2026. Out of 1,04,240 students who appeared, 19,736 passed — a pass rate of 18.93%. Kansika from Hisar and Mahek Gupta from Hyderabad jointly secured All India Rank 1, both scoring 361 out of 400. Check your scorecard at caresults.icai.org with your registration number and roll number.

If you did not clear Foundation this time, the January 2027 attempt is seven weeks away — 3, 5, 7 and 9 January. The form deadline is the same: 16 November without late fee. Do not spend a week feeling bad about the result. Read your marksheet, list the papers where you lost marks, and restart with the weakest paper first. The Foundation mock series runs in December — check the BoS portal for the paper-wise dates.

What to do this week — a short checklist

  1. Today: fill the Jan 2027 exam form on eservices.icai.org and save the acknowledgement.
  2. By 18 Nov: register for MTP Series I on bosmtp.icai.org, and tell your branch if you want physical mode.
  3. 19, 21, 23 Nov: take the three Group I mocks at 10 AM, check each the same evening, and write the mistakes in an error log.
  4. 20–22 Nov: if your form has any error, fix it in the correction window. After 22 November, no correction is possible.

Do not trust forwarded messages for dates. Exam forms, mock dates and results change only through ICAI's own announcements. If a WhatsApp forward says the date moved, check icai.org or boslive.icai.org before believing it.
